# Markets — Legal Status of Prediction Markets (US and Canada, Oct 2026)

**TL;DR**
- **Canada (where most of us are):** Polymarket is **barred in Ontario** (OSC settlement) and **self-restricted in Alberta, BC and Quebec** since July 2026. Kalshi's Canadian entry (via Wealthsimple) **excludes sports**.
- So the Polymarket tennis track may be **research-only** for Canadian members. Reading public data is fine; trading may not be.
- **US:** federal courts are **split** on whether states can regulate sports event contracts (3rd Circuit for Kalshi; 9th and 6th for the states). Three petitions are at the Supreme Court and the CFTC has a proposed rule (June 2026).
- Not legal advice. Re-check before any member trades.

**Affects:** [[Markets — Polymarket Order Book, Fees and Execution]], [[Markets — Cross-Venue Prices - Polymarket, Kalshi and Betfair]], [[Tennis — In-Play Market Research]].

## Canada

| Item | Status |
|---|---|
| **Polymarket in Ontario** | Banned through an OSC settlement. Sources disagree on the year (2023–2025); one reports a ban until April 2027 (unconfirmed) [1][2] |
| **Polymarket in AB, BC, QC** | Added to its restricted list in July 2026. This is the platform's choice, not a provincial ruling [1][3] |
| **Kalshi** | Entered Canada via Wealthsimple; economic, environmental and financial contracts only. **No sports or elections** [4] |
| **Regulators** | CIRO: products "not entitled to enter Canadian markets simply because they exist elsewhere"; warnings to get licensed or face enforcement [2][5] |

**Practical:** Ontario members shouldn't trade on Polymarket. Use public market data, paper trading and backtests. Never use VPNs to get around geo-blocks; that's also exactly what geolocation vendors detect ([[Betting Apps — Geolocation Compliance]]).

## United States

- **3rd Circuit (Apr 2026):** Kalshi's sports contracts are swaps under the CEA; federal preemption of New Jersey law [6].
- **9th Circuit (Aug 2026):** sports event contracts are **not** swaps; no preemption [6].
- **6th Circuit (Sep 2026):** the CEA does not preempt Ohio and Tennessee gambling laws [7][8].
- **CFTC:** proposed prediction-market rule on 10 Jun 2026 (the 2024 proposal was withdrawn); has sued multiple states, including New Mexico [6][9].
- **States:** Michigan civil action (Mar 2026), Arizona criminal charges, and Connecticut ordering 9 platforms to stop sports contracts [6].
- **Polymarket US** is a separate CFTC-regulated exchange with its own fee schedule ([[Markets — Polymarket Order Book, Fees and Execution]]).

## What this changes for the research

- Design everything to work on **public data + paper trading** first. The research value doesn't depend on placing bets.
- If anyone trades, it's on a venue legal in *their* jurisdiction. Betfair isn't available in most of North America either; check locally.
