# Gantry Red/Blue research hub

The existing research vault is organized here by exercise role. All 99 published research notes have at least one team or shared assignment. Cross-links are intentional: capture, accessibility and evaluation concern both teams. Original notes, Obsidian canvases and the original project board remain in place.

- [Red Team](<Red Team/README.md>): vision/OCR, action validation, capture, gantry and future model optimization.
- [Blue Team](<Blue Team/README.md>): synthetic provider design, independent identification/database scoring, contact signals and review.
- [Shared](Shared/README.md): reliable execution, experiment contracts, fixture semantics and research context.
- [Shared planning canvas](<../../Shared Gantry Planning.canvas>): exact preserved collaborative canvas; the separate root roadmap also remains.
- [Research inventory](Research%20Inventory.json): original paths, source hashes, reviewed outlines and team assignments.

The implemented loop lives in `packages/phone_lab`: deterministic fictional iPhone pages → committed PNG → Red OCR/typed observations → isolated actor database → independent Blue report. Training remains held. Red sees pixels and public receipts; labels, truth, borders and scores belong to Blue.
